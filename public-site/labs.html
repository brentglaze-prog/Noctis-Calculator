What testing is available?Baseline, metabolic & hormone markers+

We facilitate access to testing through Fullscript. Independent licensed clinicians review test requests and authorize them when appropriate. The available catalog depends on the test, your location, and the authorization pathway.

01

Baseline health

Blood counts, metabolic health, and kidney- and liver-related markers can help establish a clearer starting point.

02

Cardiometabolic health

Lipids, glucose-related markers, and other appropriate testing can add context beyond the mirror or the scale.

03

Hormones & recovery

Relevant hormone and recovery-related markers may be discussed when they fit your history, goals, and clinical context.

Availability varies by state. Fullscript’s authorization network is currently unavailable for clients in New York, New Jersey, and Rhode Island; Hawaii has a more limited test selection. Fullscript determines eligible options based on your location and the testing pathway.

How does it work?Purchase, authorization & results+
  1. 1

    Start directly with Fullscript

    Open our Fullscript link to review available testing and pricing. Complete Fullscript’s required account and health information. No NOCTIS coaching consultation is required.

  2. 2

    Complete testing through Fullscript

    An independent clinician reviews the request. If approved, follow the instructions for your test and collection location. At-home collection depends on local availability.

  3. 3

    Get your results & follow through

    Access your results and bring medical questions to your healthcare provider. If you choose NOCTIS coaching, we support education, coaching goals, and follow-through with your provider’s recommendations.

Fullscript’s authorization network handles critical-result notifications. Ongoing medical interpretation, treatment, and follow-up care are not included in that authorization service.

What is included?Lab purchases, optional coaching & medical care+

NOCTIS provides coaching, harm-reduction education, and access to lab testing through Fullscript. Our current service does not include medical lab interpretation, diagnosis, treatment, or drug-cycle management. A licensed healthcare professional must assess medical findings and make treatment decisions. Bloodwork does not establish that PED or peptide use is safe.

Lab purchases are completed through Fullscript. Review testing, authorization, and collection charges before checkout. NOCTIS coaching is optional and separate from your lab purchase. Confirm FSA/HSA eligibility with your plan administrator; eligibility for testing does not automatically cover coaching or training.
