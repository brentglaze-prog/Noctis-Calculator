Privacy Policy

NOCTIS collects only what you send us to request or receive coaching, uses it to coach you, and never sells it or uses it for advertising. NOCTIS Coaching, operated by Brent Glaze in Georgia (“NOCTIS,” “we”), runs noctiscoaching.com and the client portal at portal.noctiscoaching.com.

Effective November 1, 2026

What we collect

  • Consultation requests. Your name, email, phone number if you give one, preferred contact method, the service you’re interested in, your goal, your biggest obstacle, lab collection preference, and how you found us or who referred you.
  • Client portal. If you become a client: your training plans, check-ins, progress notes and photos, and any health information you choose to share with your coach.
  • Technical data. Our host, Cloudflare, processes your IP address, browser type and request details to deliver and protect the site. Cloudflare may set a strictly necessary security cookie.

What we don’t collect: the site has no analytics, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies. The peptide calculator and the Learning Lab run entirely in your browser; the numbers you enter are never sent to us or stored.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request and arrange a consultation.
  • To deliver coaching: plans, check-ins, feedback and scheduling.
  • To send emails you’d expect, such as confirming we received your request.
  • To keep the site and portal secure and working.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that run NOCTIS, and only for that purpose:

  • Cloudflare hosts the website, stores consultation requests and sends our emails.
  • Railway hosts the client portal and its database.
  • Square processes coaching payments outside this website; we never see your full card number.

Lab testing. Labs through Fullscript are ordered, processed and reported under Fullscript’s own terms and privacy policy. Fullscript, its clinicians and its labs handle that data, not NOCTIS. Results reach your coach only if you share them.

We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect someone’s safety. We never sell personal information or share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Requests that don’t become coaching: 24 months, then deleted.
  • Client records: 3 years after coaching ends, then deleted.
  • You can ask us to delete your information sooner (below).

Your choices

Email brent@noctiscoaching.com to see, correct, export or delete your information, or to stop emails. We’ll respond within 30 days. Deleting your information ends portal access.

Security

The site and portal use encrypted connections, and queued inquiry emails are stored encrypted. No system is perfectly secure; tell us right away if you think your account has been compromised.

Health information

NOCTIS provides coaching and education, not medical care, and is not a HIPAA covered entity. We still treat health information you share as confidential and use it only to coach you.

Age

NOCTIS services are for adults 18 and older. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone younger; if we learn we have, we delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the effective date above. If the change is significant, we’ll email current clients first.
