Coaching and clinical care: our boundaries

NOCTIS combines performance coaching with practitioner-led services where offered. Some practitioners are licensed providers; clinical services, including laboratory interpretation, are provided within their professional scope, applicable requirements, and the service relationship. Coaching is not emergency care and does not replace appropriate medical care.
