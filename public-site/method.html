Why NOCTIS exists

People come to NOCTIS for different reasons: to improve their health, get stronger, change their physique, or prepare for the stage. We built it to give them a clear plan, honest support, and accountability that fits real life.

For people already using PEDs or peptides, that also means a place to ask questions without judgment, access bloodwork through Fullscript, and prepare for open conversations with their healthcare provider.

You do not need to have everything figured out. You need a place to start.

Assess

Start with your goals, daily routines, training experience, barriers, and the support you need.

Structure

Build a focused plan with priorities that fit your schedule and capacity.

Execute

Keep the work visible through your portal, check-ins, and direct communication.

Refine

Use progress and recovery signals to make measured coaching adjustments.

Coaching and clinical care: our boundaries

NOCTIS currently provides coaching, harm-reduction education, and access to testing through Fullscript with independent clinical authorization. Medical lab interpretation, diagnosis, treatment, and ongoing clinical monitoring are not included. We encourage open communication with your treating clinician and help you follow through with their recommendations. Explore lab access →
