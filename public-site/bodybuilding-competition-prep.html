WHAT PREP LOOKS LIKE

One strategy. Every phase connected.

Prep is not a meal plan sitting beside a workout. It is a coordinated process that changes as the show gets closer.

01

Build the phase

Training, nutrition habits, cardio, recovery, and timing are aligned to where you are now.

02

See what the mirror misses

Check-ins, performance, condition, posing, and recovery guide coaching. We encourage medical review of lab findings and support follow-through with your clinician.

03

Adjust without panic

Decisions are deliberate, documented, and tied to the goal—not driven by one bad day.
