PEPTIDE CALCULATOR

Your dose.
The right measurement.

Convert a dose in milligrams (mg) or micrograms (mcg) into milliliters (mL) and the matching syringe marking. Enter the actual vial strength or labeled concentration and the dose provided by your prescriber.

Calculate your volume

CLEAR UNITS. CLEAR RESULTS.

Read the numbers correctly.

  • mg and mcg describe the amount of peptide. 1 mg = 1,000 mcg.
  • mL describes the volume of liquid.
  • U-100 syringe units describe a volume marking. 1 unit = 0.01 mL.

How the calculator works

Concentration = peptide amount ÷ final mixed volume.
Volume = dose ÷ concentration.
U-100 marking = volume in mL × 100.

Your dose comes first.

This tool calculates the volume for the dose you enter. It does not choose a peptide, recommend a dose, or create a treatment plan. Confirm your dose and vial details with your prescriber or pharmacist.

Single-ingredient, mg-based calculations only. For blends, IU-based medication, fixed-dose pens, or unclear labels, get product-specific instructions from the dispensing pharmacy.

Your entries stay in this browser tab. The calculator does not send or save them.
