My label is unclear or contains a blend

This calculator needs the amount for one peptide in mg, or its labeled mg/mL concentration. Do not add together different ingredients in a blend. IU-based products and pens need product-specific instructions. If the label is unclear, confirm it with the dispensing pharmacy.

Which unit is written with that dose?

mg and mcg are different. 1 mg = 1,000 mcg. Match the unit on your instructions.

I don’t know my dose

This calculator converts the dose you enter. It does not choose a dose or treatment. Confirm your dose and unit with your prescriber or pharmacist before calculating.

Choose your U-100 syringe
My syringe is marked in mL

Read your physical syringe. Syringes of the same size can have different line spacing.

Help me check the small lines

Find two numbered lines. Divide the difference between those numbers by the number of spaces between them. For example, from 0 to 10 units, ten spaces means 1 unit per space; five spaces means 2 units per space.

If you cannot confirm the line spacing, stop and ask your pharmacist. U-40 and U-500 syringes, pens, clicks, and medication IU are not supported here.

CHECK BEFORE CALCULATING

Do these match your label and instructions?

Pay particular attention to mg versus mcg and your vial’s strength.

How this calculator works

Concentration = peptide amount ÷ final liquid volume. Volume = dose ÷ concentration. U-100 marking = volume in mL × 100. U-100 syringe units are volume markings, not the peptide dose or medication IU.

Calculations use the dose you enter. They do not recommend a dose or establish that a product is suitable for use. Confirm product-specific directions with your prescriber or pharmacist.
